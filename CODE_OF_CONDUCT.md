# Code of Conduct

3Dica follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: we want 3Dica to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to CAD, to JavaScript or to open source.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, trolling, or publishing other people's private information.

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature on the comment or issue, or ask the maintainers in an issue for a private way to reach them, without
including details. Maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly, keep the reporter's identity private,
and act according to the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant: a correction, a warning, a temporary
ban or a permanent ban.
